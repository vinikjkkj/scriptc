THE PREDICTIONS

P1  THE HEADLINE, AND I EXPECT IT TO BE ZERO.
    The live population of the 934,576 class at the peak is UNCHANGED by the
    patch.  Before: copy(chunk) then release(chunk) -- the original dies one
    instruction later, so ONE buffer per chunk survives.  After: ref(chunk) --
    the SAME buffer survives, still ONE per chunk.  The patch removes
    TURNOVER (one alloc + one free + one 934 KB memcpy per chunk), not
    RESIDENT BYTES.
    PREDICTED: HCPKSIZE 934576 reads 6-7 on BOTH arms, and the peak delta on
    pkCrtBusy is consistent with zero.
    The 5.61-6.54 MB figure carried by the slice that wrote the patch is
    predicted here to be a FLOW counted as a POPULATION -- the same error this
    front made two slices ago and that 1:1 in six runs refuted.

P2  THE HALF OF P1 MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG, NAMED: TRANSIENT OVERLAP.
    During the memcpy the original and the copy are BOTH live.  If the peak
    snapshot lands in that window, PRE can read 12-14 in the 934,576 class
    where POS reads 6-7.  The sampler is a 5 ms poll on a 1 MiB step, so it
    may or may not catch it.
    PREDICTED: PRE reads 6-7, not 12-14.  If PRE reads 12-14 even once, P1 is
    wrong and the overlap is real.
    THE DECIDING ITEM IS THE PER-RUN HCPKSIZE 934576 COUNT, NOT ANY t.

P3  FRAGMENTATION.  The alloc+free pair of 934 KB per chunk can move
    free / uncommitted even with busy equal.
    PREDICTED: free and uncommitted at the peak do NOT separate at n=3.

P4  THE SETTLED CENSUS IS A NEGATIVE CONTROL.  The lane effect on settled
    busy in evidence-peakvar was |t| = 0.01.  If settled does not separate
    here either, that is COHERENCE, not a new null.
    PREDICTED: settled busy does not separate.
