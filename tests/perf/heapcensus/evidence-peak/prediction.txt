FR2 -- registered BEFORE the run, so the reading cannot be a story told afterwards
=================================================================================
written 2026-10-11, after the build was launched and before any census existed

THE QUESTION
  What is the largest occupant of the CRT heap AT THE PEAK, on the STACKLESS
  lane, on CHUNKS=8 CONVS=400 MSGS=6 TEXTLEN=300 ROUNDS=1 IDLE_S=60.
  The uninstrumented subject's peak CRT heap is 53.438 MiB (vmcensus v3).
  THIS BINARY'S number will be larger and is NOT that number; see DENOMINATOR.

WHAT I EXPECT, in order, and why
  1. HCPKSIZE 65536 -- the 64 KiB arena chunks (cycle arena scr_cycle.c, and
     the string arena scr_string.c, which has chunks of the same shape).
     At SETTLED on the FIBER lane the committed census read 164 blocks /
     10.75 MB. cycstat on app182 read peakheld=276 chunks for the CYCLE
     arena alone, which would be 18.1 MB at its own peak. So I expect this
     row to be the largest single row, somewhere in 150-350 blocks.
     CROSS-CHECK AVAILABLE: cycstat's peakheld is an INDEPENDENT counter of
     part of this row and the two must be consistent.
  2. A handful of ~1,048,576 B blocks. vmcensus v3 saw SIX one-region bases
     of 1.004 MiB at the peak, and big.census.txt proves that shape is what
     8 mallocs of 1,048,000 B build. So ~6 blocks of about a megabyte,
     ~6.3 MB in total.
  3. SQLite. WHICH-BUFFER established the 1,296-1,352 band is SQLite's and
     that the 4,376 class "is the whole database". Its page cache is one
     large allocation and may BE item 2.
  4. The small-block population (16/24/32/40/48/64/96) in the thousands,
     each row a few hundred KB -- large in COUNT and small in BYTES.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS A NULL, and a null is the result if it happens
  * If the largest occupant is LIVE DATA, that is a null and it is the
    answer. The cycle arena already closed that way today at 62.93% live.
    OCCUPIED IS NOT A DEFECT and I will not promote a survivor to a leak.
  * If the peak heap is mostly BUSY rather than mostly free, there is no
    fragmentation story at the peak, whatever the settled heap looks like.
    At settled the fiber arm read busy 15.49 MiB against free 29.69 MiB.
    If the peak inverts that, the settled fragmentation is a CONSEQUENCE of
    the decline, not a cause of the peak.

THE DENOMINATOR, stated in advance because it is the thing most likely to be
misquoted afterwards
  * The ranking's denominator is HCPKTOTAL bytes -- the CRT heap's BUSY
    bytes in THIS INSTRUMENTED BINARY at the snapshot instant.
  * It is NOT 53.438 MiB. WHICH-BUFFER.md ss.5 forbids quoting a commit or
    RSS figure from an instrumented binary and that prohibition is kept.
  * COVERAGE (snapshot PrivateUsage / kernel PeakPagefileUsage) is what
    licenses calling this instant "the peak". Below ~99% it is a trough.

COSTS DECLARED IN ADVANCE
  * two 16,384-row tables, 786,432 B of .data, measured off the PE against
    fr1-out/S-plain -- which is the SAME compiler and runtime source tree
    (231efb466 / 6a19e80ea) and differs ONLY by SCRIPTC_PROF_CFLAGS.
  * one poller thread, 64 KiB reserved stack.
  * the walks: HCPKCOST prints count, total ms and max ms.
  * ABBA, four runs of ONE binary, trigger ARMED vs SCR_HEAPCEN_PEAK_OFF=1,
    comparing the kernel's own peakPagefile, answers whether the instrument
    moved the peak it measures.
