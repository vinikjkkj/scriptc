WHY THE `sa` ARM LANDED ON THE OTHER LANE
  `sa.build-env.txt` records `worktree /g/scriptc` and names no KNOB. The
  recipe did not set SCRIPTC_STACKLESS -- and that is exactly the problem.
  The default flipped to STACKLESS at `8f4100d7a`, 2026-10-09 19:46 -0300.
  `G:\scriptc`'s `dist/` at the time of this build predated the lane
  entirely (`packages/compiler/dist/ir/coro-plans.js` did not exist; `rg -l
  SCRIPTC_STACKLESS packages/*/dist` read 0 files against 8 in a fresh
  worktree -- `evidence-peak/lane-dist.txt`). A compiler that cannot read
  the knob cannot honour the flip, so "knob absent" delivered FIBER where
  the shipping default was STACKLESS. There is no variable in the build
  command to denounce this: the command is correct and the compiler is old.

  START on `sa.build-env.txt` is 2026-10-11T02:50:03Z = 2026-10-10 23:50
  local, which is AFTER the flip. Before the flip the stale `dist/` and the
  source agreed (both fiber), so the same recipe run on 2026-10-08 would
  carry no defect at all. The flip is the hinge.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT TOUCH -- the scope is narrow on purpose
  DOES NOT TOUCH. Anything measured inside the `sa` run against itself. The
  disappearance of `HCSIZE 131079`, `HCSIZE 65536` reading 164 blocks, the
  113/47/4 ownership split, and the C1/C2 translation-unit controls are all
  one-binary facts. The chunk size moving 131,079 -> 65,536 is source
  arithmetic and is lane-independent in both directions.

  DOES TOUCH. Every figure that crosses from `sa` to the `evidence-idle`
  baseline, because those two binaries are on different lanes. Concretely:
  `sa` reports CRT busy 16,243,072 B and the baseline `evidence-idle/on`
  reports 18,907,440 B, and part of that 2.66 MB gap is the lane rather
  than the string-arena repair.

HOW LARGE THE CONFOUND COULD BE, AND THE HONEST LIMIT ON THAT NUMBER
  `pagecensus/evidence-fr2/DECOMP.txt`, block "WHAT THE LANE REMOVED",
  row `CRT HEAP`: 79.828 MiB on the fiber arm against a 58.711 MiB
  stackless mean, delta -21.117 MiB. That is 7.2x the 2.9375 MiB the
  string-arena repair claims.

  BUT THAT IS AT THE PEAK, and `sa` is a settled census taken through
  `/shutdown` after 45 s of settle and 60 s of idle. The lane's effect on
  the SETTLED CRT heap has never been measured in this repository. So the
  confound is proven to exist and is NOT proven to be large here. Stating
  it as "21 MiB of error" would be the same kind of error this file is
  about: a right measurement carried past its scope.

WHAT THIS RESOLVES IN `POSTFIX-CENSUS.md` SECTION 5
  The marked sentence was: "The two agree to 0.004 percentage points by two

  denominator. Both routes re-run with numerator and denominator on ONE lane:

    ROUTE A  this run against its own counterfactual pre-fix heap

    ROUTE B  this run against the stackless baseline evidence-idle/on
             3,080,192 / 18,907,440                            = 16.291%
             agreement: 0.065 pp

  The two routes still agree, at 0.065 pp rather than 0.004 pp -- sixteen
  times looser, and real. BOTH figures are now ~16.2-16.3%, where the
  retracted pair read 15.940% and 15.944%; the shift is the denominator
  moving to a same-lane heap, not the numerator, which did not move at all.

  NOTE ON ROUTE B's REMAINING IMPURITY, stated rather than buried: route B's
  denominator is a DIFFERENT RUN on the same lane (evidence-idle/on, a
  different binary at 21facf366 without the repair). It is same-lane, which
  is what was broken; it is not same-run, which route A is and route B
  cannot be. Route A remains the only figure whose numerator and denominator
  come from one process.

WHAT THIS RUN COSTS THE REGISTERED PREDICTION, stated plainly
  `P2a` -- "HCSIZE 65536 reads 164" -- now has one pass (`sa`, fiber) and one

================================================================================

  The lines this band answers, in the section "THE CONFOUND THIS FILE
  FLAGGED, NOW MEASURED -- `sb.compare.txt`":

      "busy             16,243,072     15,902,592      -340,480 B  (-0.325 MiB)"

      "AND THE LANE IS NOT A SMALL THING IN THIS HEAP. It is worth -23.3 MiB

  THIS FILE WAS ALREADY THE MOST CAREFUL OF THE FOUR. Its "WHAT THE PAIR
  CANNOT SAY" paragraph ends "It is not a point estimate of -340,480", and

  THE MEASUREMENT, 2026-10-11, evidence-lane/ -- 3 runs per arm, alternated
  CBBCCB, order declared in evidence-lane/PREREG.txt before run 1 existed.
  Same two exes (sa2-out182-prof FIBER, sb-out182-prof STACKLESS), same ten
  knobs, nothing built. Full table: evidence-lane/RESULTS.txt, reproducible
  with `node evidence-lane/lane-stats.mjs`.
  
    arm C (FIBER)     busy sd    239,808 B   range 440,448 B over three runs
    arm B (STACKLESS) busy sd     37,880 B   range  65,856 B over three runs
    measured lane busy delta (B - C) = -98,859 B
    |t| = 0.71, Welch df = 2.10, t_crit = 4.303  =>  NOT SEPARABLE FROM ZERO

  THE CLAIM IS NOT REFUTED; IT IS UNSUPPORTED. The pair never had the power to
  assert it. Power to detect a true 340,480 B lane shift at n=3 here is 3.0%;
  the minimum detectable effect at 80% power is 721,173 B; n = 7 per arm
  (about 33 min) would settle it. The fiber arm's own busy range, 440,448 B
  across three runs of ONE binary at fixed knobs, is LARGER than the effect
  the single pair was read as showing.

  AND THE WIDER SENTENCE IN THIS SECTION IS BOUNDED THE SAME WAY. The claim
  that the lane's settled effect is "large" and "lives almost entirely outside
  the busy column" rests on -7.919 MiB of free and -23.281 MiB of uncommitted
  from the same single pair. Measured 3 v 3:
  
    free          delta -12,833,904 B  |t| 4.84  df 2.22  t_crit 4.303  marginal
    uncommitted   delta  -9,243,307 B  |t| 1.48  df 2.04  t_crit 4.303  NOT sep.
    regions       FIBER 10, 11, 10  vs  STACKLESS 9, 9, 9
  
  The fiber arm's uncommitted sd is 10,794,421 B -- comparable to the claimed
  -24,412,160 B itself -- so that column cannot carry a point estimate from a
  pair either. The DIRECTION survives: all three fiber runs settle with more
  free and more regions than all three stackless runs. The MAGNITUDES do not.
  The honest form of the wider sentence is "the fiber lane settles with a
  structurally larger heap, direction reproduced, size unpinned".
  
  A FLOOR THAT APPLIES TO THE "marginal" ROW ABOVE AND TO EVERY SIMILAR ONE.
  At 3 v 3 there are C(6,3) = 20 arm assignments, so the smallest attainable
  two-sided permutation p is 2/20 = 0.10. No effect at this n can reach
  p < 0.05 without the normal assumption, and the free/committed rows revert
  to "not separable" under the pre-registered leave-one-out. Read them as
  "worth n=7", not as established.

  WHAT THIS FILE GOT RIGHT AND KEEPS. The refusal to carry the peak slice's
  -21.117 MiB into a settled-heap error bar stands, and stands by a wider
  margin than before: the 95% CI on the settled busy delta tops out at
  702,009 B. The "WHAT IS STILL NOT CLOSED" item -- "Nothing here licenses
  applying -340,480 B ... to any other arm or knob set" -- is now stronger
  than it was written: nothing licenses applying it to THIS arm and THIS

  SECTIONS OF THIS FILE NOT NAMED ABOVE ARE NOT THEREBY IN FORCE -- they are

  It does not touch the string-arena repair's -3,020,443 B busy delta, which
