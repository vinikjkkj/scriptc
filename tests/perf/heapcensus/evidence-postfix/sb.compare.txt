    THE HEADLINE. On the BUSY column the lane is worth -340,480 B =
    -0.325 MiB, which is 11.1% of the 2.9375 MiB the string-arena repair
    claims -- NOT the 7.2x the peak-slice figure would have suggested.
    The -21.117 MiB that pagecensus/evidence-fr2/DECOMP.txt measures at
    the PEAK does not transfer to the settled busy heap.

    AND THE REST OF THE HEAP SAYS THE OPPOSITE, WHICH IS THE FINDING.
    free is -7.92 MiB and uncommitted is -23.28 MiB, for a committed
    total (busy + free) of -8.244 MiB and 2 fewer regions.
    The lane's settled-heap effect is large, and it lives almost entirely
    OUTSIDE the busy column: the fibre arm settles holding 23 MiB more
    uncommitted address space and 8 MiB more free-but-committed heap at
    nearly the same busy total. That is consistent with the peak delta
    being real, and it locates it in space the busy census does not count.

    SO BOTH HALVES OF THE BRIEF'S FORK WERE PARTLY RIGHT. The lane was
    'mostly hygiene' FOR THE NUMBER THIS CENSUS REPORTS, and it was a
    large structural difference in the heap that number is drawn from.

=== WHAT THIS PAIR CANNOT SAY ===
    It is ONE pair, so it carries no variance estimate. The committed
    arms vary by hundreds of busyBlocks at fixed knobs (47,606 / 47,624 /
    47,719 / 47,940 across half-on, on, sa, sb), and sb holds 221 MORE
    busy blocks than sa while holding 340,480 FEWER busy bytes, so the
    block composition moved as well as the total. A -340,480 B delta from
    a single pair cannot be separated from run-to-run jitter here, and no
    committed arm repeats THIS arm's knobs on THIS lane to bound it.
    What the pair does support is a CEILING of the right order: the lane
    is not worth 21 MiB on the settled busy heap, and the confound
    LANE.txt flagged is a fraction of the claimed effect, not a multiple
    of it.
