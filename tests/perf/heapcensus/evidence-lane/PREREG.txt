THE ORDER, DECLARED NOW: C B B C C B
------------------------------------
Chosen, with the reason, before run 1:

  (a) It is evidence-variance's ABBAAB with A -> C, term for term. That
      makes the position structure IDENTICAL to the slice whose numbers
      this one is being read against, so the two are comparable and the
      pre-registered leave-one-out carries over unchanged.

THE B ARM IS RE-RUN IN THIS SESSION, DELIBERATELY
-------------------------------------------------
The three b-runs committed in evidence-variance use the SAME sb binary at
the SAME knobs. They are NOT reused as this slice's arm. They are a
different session, and session is exactly the term this slice cannot hold
fixed across a reuse. Re-running B here buys a third datum for free: the
BETWEEN-SESSION drift of one fixed binary, which nobody has measured. It
will be reported whichever way it falls.

------------------------------------
  P1  The lane's busy delta SURVIVES in sign and order of magnitude
      (-300,000 to -400,000 B, C minus B positive i.e. fiber heavier) and
      IS SEPARABLE at the Welch df. Basis: arm B's busy sd is 762 B and the

      closing "47 was a draw" on BOTH lanes.

THE HALF OF THIS DESIGN MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG: P1, by way of ARM C's
VARIANCE, not by way of the effect. The tight 762 B sd belongs to the
STACKLESS arm, where almost nothing parks. The FIBER arm carries 20,000+
task stacks and its busy distribution may be far wider. If arm C's sd is of
the order of hundreds of KB, the Welch df collapses and -340,480 B becomes
not-separable exactly as the committed column did.

IF THAT IS WHAT HAPPENS, THAT IS THE RESULT, and it is worth more than a
confirmation: it means the settled heap does not distinguish the lanes at
n=3, and the sentence must be retracted in the three documents named above.
EITHER WAY, EACH ARM'S SD IS REPORTED BEFORE ANY t.

THE CRITICAL VALUE IS TAKEN AT THE WELCH df AND PRINTED
-------------------------------------------------------
The Welch df collapses to ~2 when the variances are unequal, and the
two-sided 95% bar there is 4.303, NOT the 2.776 of df=4. Reading the table
at df=4 flipped a verdict in the previous slice. The df and the critical
value used appear in every row of every table.

ERROR BARS GO ON THE MEASURED FACTOR
------------------------------------

goes on N, the measured factor, not on the unit, which is a definition.
