WHAT ONE SUSPENDED TASK COSTS ON THE FIBER LANE
Denominator: 20207 stacks on f1 minus 17 on v3 = 20190 tasks, both LOW mode.
This is a DIFFERENCE per task, not a measurement of one task in isolation:
it is what the process stops paying when the lane stops making stacks.

  term                      MiB total   B per task   share
  STACK, PAGE_GUARD           236.602        12288    60.7%
  STACK, usable commit         87.366         4537    22.4%
  page tables (residue)        39.426         2048    10.1%
  CRT heap                     26.390         1371     6.8%
  image/mapped/small           -0.273          -14    -0.1%
  TOTAL                       389.512        20229   100.0%

The 12,288 B guard row is the kernel constant estado-vmwalk sec.7 names, and
it alone is 60.7% of the per-task cost. sec.7 says no knob reaches it and
that the only thing that removes it is not having a stack per suspended
task. That is what the stackless lane does, and this is the bill it stops.
