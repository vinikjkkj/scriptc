MANIFEST -- the SHIPPED binary's private commit AT REST, decomposed by base,
            and what the two heap instruments disagree about
========================================================================
date        2026-10-11
question    (1) what are the ~20.5 MiB the shipped app182 binary commits at
                rest made of, and how much of it is CRT heap
            (2) do the 43 free blocks >= 64 KiB (15.17 MiB) that the
                in-process census reports at rest exist in the product

WHY THIS DIRECTORY EXISTS AT ALL
  evidence-fr2 decomposed the PEAK with the same external walker and says so
  in its own "WHAT IS NOT HERE". There was no rest decomposition, because
  vmcensus.c keeps only its BEST walk and the best walk is by construction
  the peak. vmrest.c adds a timestamped series and a periodic full dump and
  removes nothing, so peak and rest now come out of ONE run.

THE INSTANT, taken from the harness and not from anyone's prose
  memrig.mts:467-470 sleeps SETTLE_MS (default 45,000) after SYNC-DONE, marks
  SETTLED-r<n>, then sleeps 30 s twice marking IDLE-30s and IDLE-60s.
  THE REST WINDOW IS SETTLED-r1 .. IDLE-60s. It is the same window the pmon
  series fr1-run/a1..a6 was read in, and in all six runs here the pmon
  privateCommit column is FLAT TO THE BYTE across 229-232 samples of it.

THE INSTRUMENTS
  tests/perf/pagecensus/vmrest.c     out-of-process VirtualQueryEx walk.
    = vmcensus.c with a timestamped SERIES line per walk and a full
      decomposition dump every Nth walk added. Its walk, its classification
      and its emit() are vmcensus.c's, unchanged; the diff deletes eight
      lines and all eight are argument parsing, the output-file open, and
      message text. The subject is unmodified and has no instrument inside
      it, so its rest is its own.
  tests/perf/pagecensus/heapsplit.c  a CONTROL, not a subject instrument:
      for every free block HeapWalk reports, ask VirtualQuery whether those
      pages are committed.
  tests/perf/pagecensus/bsstgt.c     a CONTROL for the claim that a large
      .bss is charged to PrivateUsage with its copy-on-write untaken, and
      therefore lands in vmcensus's RESIDUE.
  built with zig 0.16.0 from G:\tools\zig (NOT the PATH's Chocolatey
  0.15.2), target x86_64-unknown-windows-gnu, asserted by
  `zig cc -print-target-triple` in the session that built them.

THE COLUMN
  PROCESS_MEMORY_COUNTERS_EX.PrivateUsage, read per walk. PRIVATE COMMIT.
  It is NOT workingSet and it is NOT PeakWorkingSet; both are printed
  beside it in every dump so the two cannot be confused.

CONTROLS -- each is a run in this directory, not a sentence
  ctl-pos.txt        vmrest against tests/perf/pagecensus/vmtgt.c:
                     256 MiB committed and untouched reads PRIVATE non-stack
                     256.164 MiB with residue 0.766 while the working set
                     stays 8.1 MiB (it measures commit, not residency);
                     zero fibers reads 2 stacks, not 0 and not 2,000.
  ctl-bss.txt        a 40 MiB file-scope array at four touch levels.
                     PrivateUsage is 40.62 MiB in ALL FOUR, including the one
                     that never writes a byte of it. IMAGE cow-taken moves
                     0.25 -> 10.21 -> 20.17 -> 40.09 MiB and RESIDUE moves
                     40.13 -> 30.16 -> 20.20 -> 0.27 MiB, 1:1 with how much
                     was touched. The charge is taken at map time; the COW
                     discriminator cannot see it; it lands in RESIDUE.
  ctl-heapsplit.txt  four heap shapes. In the arm where NOTHING is freed,
                     free blocks >= 64 KiB are 0.961 MiB and 100% MEM_COMMIT
                     and busy+free is WITHIN PrivateUsage -- the detector is
                     not biased. In the three arms that free, 74.6% / 93.7% /
                     92.7% of those bytes are MEM_RESERVE, and busy+free
                     EXCEEDS the whole process's PrivateUsage by up to
                     25.1 MiB, which is impossible for committed memory.

WHAT IS NOT HERE
  The busy/free split of the CLEAN binary. It cannot be had from outside --
  VirtualQueryEx sees committed pages, not heap blocks -- and the in-process
  column that would give it is the one TWO-INSTRUMENTS.md refutes. The honest
  next instrument is named there, with its cost.

LINE ENDINGS, declared rather than discovered later
  Every prose file and every script in this directory is LF, CR=0. The CRs
  that do exist are in MACHINE-WRITTEN artifacts and are the programs' own:
  *.vm.txt (vmrest, fopen "w" on Windows), *.rss.csv (pmon.exe),
  r3/r4.heapcen.txt (scr_heap_census.h), and the captured stdout in
  TABLE.txt, ctl-*.txt and szprobe.txt. This matches the convention the
  heapcensus evidence directories already follow.

THE COMMITTED vmrest.c WAS RECOMPILED AFTER ITS HEADER COMMENT WAS WRITTEN
  and still builds clean, so the file in the tree is the file that was run,
  modulo the comment. The runs in this directory were produced by the
  identical code with the shorter header.
